About StoryCast

Our mission

StoryCast shares audio and video stories that everyone can enjoy, whatever their abilities.

Accessibility features

  • Transcripts for every audio story
  • Captions for video
  • Full keyboard navigation with visible focus
  • High-contrast colors that meet WCAG AA
  • Semantic structure for screen readers

How to engage

Listen on the Story page, read the transcript, and explore related stories from the Home page.
